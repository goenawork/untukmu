Dear you,

If you're reading this, then you're 17 now.

It's strange to imagine. When I wrote these words, you were still at the beginning of your story.

I don't know who you became. I don't know what changed, what stayed, what made you laugh, or what made you cry.

But I hope, wherever life has taken you, you know one thing:

You were loved long before you knew how to ask for it.

See you at 17.
